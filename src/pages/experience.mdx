### Full-Stack Developer at FedEx
###### August 2018 - September 2026

#### Lead Developer

As a senior developer at FedEx, I worked as a lead of an Agile scrum team. I worked with business and architects to
understand requirements and refine features to be worked by the team. I take ownership of various features, leading
the planning and implementation. I bring technical topics and best practices to the team for review and information
sharing.

#### Frontend

I designed and built several internal React web applications. These applications span from simple data input forms
to complex geographical tools (using ArcGIS) that allowed users to create, edit, and save drawn coordinate areas.

I worked on a large-scale Android application (multiple teams working on the same codebase). These applications, in the
hands of tens of thousands of FedEx drivers, are used for the full delivery and pickup workflow. I worked on
barcode scanning, Bluetooth, label printing, and photo capture functionalities in addition to more standard design.

#### Backend

I developed and designed high volume Java (Spring Boot) microservices and distributed systems. I have built RESTful
APIs, utilized async messaging (JMS), relational databases (Oracle), and noSQL databases (Redis and Cassandra).

I built Spring Boot starters, making it easier for our team to implement common boilerplate across our applications
with simple configuration.

I inherited an application from another team and identified areas of improvement. I designed and proposed an application
rewrite to business and architecture and was given the green light. We began using in-memory and Redis caching, we
collapsed microservices that were too finely defined, and we applied compression before storing large blob data.
The combined changes lowered the application footprint by 75%!
